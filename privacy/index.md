### Privacy

**Your kart data is yours alone.** We don't collect your information. Your weight distributions, setup configurations, and presets stay private on your device—always.

#### Optional Usage Data

To help us catch bugs and improve KartBalance, you can choose to share anonymous usage data. **This is completely optional** and controlled by you.

**What we collect (only if you opt in):**
- Crash reports with technical diagnostics (no personal data)
- Anonymous app usage patterns (which features you use, not what data you enter)
- Device model and iOS version for compatibility testing

**What we never collect:**
- Your kart weights, setups, or preset configurations
- Any personal information (name, email, location, photos)
- Anything that identifies you as an individual

**You're in control:**
Toggle "Share usage data" in Settings → Preferences to enable or disable this anytime. When off, no data leaves your device.

We use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Google) for optional data collection, both of which meet Apple's privacy standards. Learn more: [firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
